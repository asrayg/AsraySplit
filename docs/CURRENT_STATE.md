# Current state and gap audit

Updated: 2026-10-20

## What exists

- SwiftUI iPhone target generated by XcodeGen.
- Dashboard, groups, group detail, activity, profile, expense detail, and settlement screens.
- Trip creation fields for dates, travelers, currency, and budget.
- Manual expenses with equal, exact, percentage, and share-based inputs.
- Cash/card/bank/Venmo/other payment labels and custom tags.
- Receipt camera/library input, Apple Vision OCR fallback, editable items, member assignment, proportional tax/tip distribution.
- Supabase migration draft, private receipt bucket, REST client, anonymous auth attempt, and AI Edge Function draft.
- Local JSON snapshot and seeded demo data.
- A single OCR parser unit test.
- Device build and test-build compilation currently succeed with Xcode 15.2.

## What is prototype-only or incorrect

### Data and money

- Models use `Double`; rounding, zero-decimal currencies, and reconciliation are unsafe.
- The client mutates `group.balance`; this can drift and cannot support concurrent users.
- Receipt totals do not enforce subtotal/tax/tip/discount reconciliation.
- Editing or deleting an expense does not reverse prior balance effects.
- Settlements are modeled as ordinary expenses instead of a distinct ledger event.
- Existing Codable snapshots are not versioned and can silently fall back to seed data.

### Backend and collaboration

- There is no durable session refresh/keychain storage or real onboarding.
- Supabase writes are mostly fire-and-forget; failures are swallowed.
- There is no backend fetch, pagination, Realtime subscription, retry queue, or conflict strategy.
- Group invitees are names, not authenticated memberships.
- There are no invite links/codes, acceptance flow, roles, member removal, or ownership transfer.
- `group_id`/`paid_by` integrity is not enforced consistently.
- Current RLS policies have not been tested and membership policy recursion must be reviewed.
- The database has no idempotency keys, revision columns, soft-delete/audit strategy, or transactional expense writer.
- The Edge Function has no rate limit, usage quota, structured observability, image normalization, or provider fallback.

### Product gaps

- No edit expense flow, search/filter implementation, currency conversion, CSV export, recurring expenses, or default splits.
- No actual debt simplification or suggested settlement graph.
- No receipt gallery/download lifecycle.
- No trip archive/leave/delete/member-management flows.
- No universal links, share sheet invitation, QR invite, or deep-link router.
- “Insights” are hard-coded presentation, not derived data.
- Notifications are local only and do not announce changes from other members.
- No support/help, report/block flow, terms, privacy policy, or account deletion.

### Engineering and release gaps

- Views are large and logic-heavy; there is no repository/use-case boundary.
- No structured error model, loading states, retry UI, telemetry, crash reporting, or feature flags.
- Almost no tests; no UI, integration, RLS, Edge Function, snapshot, accessibility, or performance tests.
- No CI/CD, staging environment, secrets strategy, release configuration, app icon, screenshots, localization, or App Store metadata.
- No privacy manifest, data-retention policy, or App Privacy answers.
- Accessibility, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, contrast, reduced motion, and localization have not been audited.

## Do not build on these assumptions

- Do not treat the current `AppStore` as a production data layer.
- Do not preserve generated UUIDs for people who are not real authenticated users; introduce `trip_participants` so invited guests can exist before account linking.
- Do not sync the local demo seed to production.
- Do not add more balance mutations to patch incorrect totals. Replace them with ledger-derived queries/RPCs.
- Do not ship anonymous auto-account creation without in-app account deletion.
