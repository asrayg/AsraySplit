Asray Split Support

Joining a trip

Open the invite link on your iPhone, or in the app tap + → Join with code and enter the 8-character code. Invites expire and can be revoked by the trip’s admins — ask for a new one if yours doesn’t work.

Working offline

Expenses and payments you add offline are saved on your iPhone and sync automatically when you reconnect. A cloud icon marks changes that haven’t synced. If a change can’t be saved, Settings → Sync shows why and lets you retry or discard it.

“Someone else made changes”

If two people edit the same expense, the app never silently overwrites either edit. You’ll be asked to keep their version or yours.

Currencies

Each trip has one currency for balances. Expenses in other currencies are converted with the exchange rate saved on that expense (ECB reference rate or one you enter), so old totals never change when rates move.

Receipt scanning

Review every item before saving. Highlighted fields were hard to read. If scanning isn’t available, the app reads the receipt on your iPhone or you can type items.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account. See the privacy policy for exactly what is deleted.
