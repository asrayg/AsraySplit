Asray Split Privacy Policy

Effective: [publication date] · Draft for legal review before launch.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address or Apple ID relay address, and the display name you choose.
  • Trip data: trips you create or join, the people in them, expenses, items, payments you record, tags and notes. Other members of a trip can see this data.
  • Receipts: receipt photos or PDFs you upload and the text extracted from them. Photos are resized on your device and location/camera metadata is removed before upload.
  • Device: a push-notification token, app version, locale and time zone, so we can deliver notifications.
  • Service records: timestamps and outcomes of receipt scans (never the receipt contents) to enforce fair-use limits and diagnose failures.

We do not use advertising identifiers, do not track you across other apps or websites, and do not sell personal data.

How we use it

Only to provide the app: syncing trips between members, calculating balances, reading receipts you choose to scan, sending notifications you enable, preventing abuse, and support.

Receipt scanning and AI

When you scan a receipt, the image is sent from our servers to an AI service provider (currently OpenAI) to extract line items. The request contains only the image — not your name, email or trip details — and is sent with data retention for training disabled. Results are suggestions you review before anything is saved. If you’re offline or prefer not to use AI, the app can read receipts on your device or you can enter items manually.

Service providers

  • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage, server functions)
  • OpenAI (receipt text extraction)
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, push notifications)
  • European Central Bank reference rates via Frankfurter (exchange rates; no personal data is sent)

Retention and deletion

  • Trip data is kept until the trip is deleted by its owner or you delete your account.
  • You can delete any receipt image you uploaded at any time; it is removed from storage.
  • Delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete account. This deletes your profile, devices, the receipt images you uploaded, and trips only you belong to. Shared trips are transferred to another member and your name in them is replaced with “Former traveler” so the remaining members’ balances stay correct.
  • Invite brute-force logs are kept 7 days; delivered notification records 30 days.

Your rights

You can export everything we hold about you in the app (Settings → Export my data), correct your profile, and delete your account. Depending on where you live you may have further rights (for example under GDPR or CCPA); contact us to exercise them.

Children

Asray Split is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
